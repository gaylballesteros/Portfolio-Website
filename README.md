Project Description

This project is a personal portfolio website created to introduce myself and showcase my educational background, professional skills, hobbies, interests, and career goals. The website presents my experience in insurance operations, customer service, automation quality assurance, and technology in a simple and interactive portfolio format.

The website also includes interactive sections where visitors can explore my hobbies and interests, view photos, and listen to music.

Features of the Website

Personal Profile – Introduction and professional background
About Me – Overview of my skills, experience, and interests
Education– Displays my academic background
Skills – Showcases my insurance, automation, QA, customer service, and research skills
Hobbies & Interests- Includes Music, K-pop, Travel, and Food
Interactive Interest Cards – Visitors can click each interest to view additional content
Music Player – Allows visitors to listen to a selected song
Photo Gallery – Displays photos related to my interests
Career Goals – Presents my professional goals and future objectives
Contact Information – Provides email, phone, and LinkedIn contact details
Downloadable CV – here you can download my CV directly from the website

Technologies Used

CSS
